About the Author

Jonathan Kent is a writer and journalist. He’s been a political reporter, a foreign correspondent and a commentator, has reported for some of the world’s great news organisations including the BBC, Reuters and Newsweek and written for many others including The Guardian and The Daily Telegraph. His 2012 series for BBC Radio 4, Our Daily Bread, was widely praised. He’s currently working on a documentary about the experiences of young British-born Chinese people. He read philosophy and theology at Keble College, Oxford and lives in the High Weald in Sussex.
